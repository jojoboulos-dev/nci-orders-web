NCI Orders — Privacy and terms

For the NCI Orders web app, used by the pediatric oncology team at the National Cancer Institute, Cairo.

Privacy

  • Who may use it. Only physicians of the pediatric oncology ward with whom the ward's NCI ORDERS folder on Google Drive has been shared.
  • What the app reads and writes. When you sign in with Google, the app asks for access to Google Drive and Google Sheets. It uses that access only to read and save the ward's own file in the NCI ORDERS folder and to add bookings to the ward's procedures sheet. It does not open, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Google account. The app reads your email address only to show which account is signed in. It does not keep your Google password; Google's own sign-in handles it.
  • Where the data is. The ward's data stays in the ward's Google Drive file, and a working copy is kept in your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to the app's developer or to anyone else, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.
  • Removing access. Signing out, or removing your account from the shared folder, ends the app's access. You can also revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps.

Terms of use

  • NCI Orders is a tool for the ward's physicians. It helps prepare orders and handovers; every order and every decision remains the responsibility of the treating physician.
  • Use it only for patients under your care, and keep your device locked.
  • The app is provided as it is, for the ward's internal use.
